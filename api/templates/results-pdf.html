Understanding the Scale

Scores closer to 0 typically reflect a preference for stability and capital preservation, where protecting what you have matters more than maximizing growth. Scores closer to 100 tend to indicate comfort with significant market volatility and a focus on long-term wealth accumulation, even when that means accepting substantial short-term fluctuations. Neither approach is better or worse. They represent different priorities, timeframes, and emotional relationships with uncertainty.
